I have worked on OpenStack platforms since 2016 and, for more than five years, on their operation in production: deployment and administration, incident management, monitoring and log management, upgrades, and the organisation that has to exist around a platform once a business depends on it. As a consultant, I have carried out these missions for banks and public administrations in France.

My interest is less in getting OpenStack to work than in keeping it under control afterwards — visibility, stability, recoverability, and the decisions that preserve them.
